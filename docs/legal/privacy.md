# Privacy

**This page summarizes how GiftSpark handles your data in plain language.** It mirrors the data declarations the app makes for app stores. The authoritative, in-app privacy policy lives within the [GiftSpark app](https://giftspark.aoneahsan.com); this documentation summary is here so the same information is easy to find.

## What data is collected

| Data | Why | Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Account and app functionality — how you appear in the feed and directory. | Yes |
| **Email address** | Account identity via Google sign-in. | Yes |
| **App interactions** | Analytics — to understand and improve how features are used. | Yes |
| **Device or other IDs** | Analytics and app functionality. | Yes |
| **Crash logs** | Reliability — to find and fix errors. | — |

Optional profile details you choose to add (such as your birthday, interests, or style) power events and gift recommendations, and you control their visibility.

## How data is used and shared

- **Used** to run the platform: recognition, rewards, events, surveys, and gifting.
- **Encrypted in transit** over HTTPS/TLS.
- **Shared with operational providers** that process data on GiftSpark's behalf — analytics (Firebase, Amplitude, Microsoft Clarity) and error tracking (Sentry) — plus delivery services you enable, such as push (OneSignal) and chat (Slack, Microsoft Teams).

GiftSpark does not sell your personal data.

## Permissions

The mobile app requests only what its features need — for example, push-notification permission for reminders and biometric access for unlock. Each sensitive capability is tied to a real feature and used only for that purpose.

## Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and associated data. The in-app account/data-deletion flow is the canonical path; for help, contact **aoneahsan@gmail.com**.

## Your rights

Depending on your region (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Use the in-app deletion flow or the contact above to exercise them.

## Honest framing

This is a readable summary, not a substitute for the full legal privacy policy presented in the app. Where this summary and the in-app policy differ in detail, the in-app policy governs.
