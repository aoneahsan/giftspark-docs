# Members & Teams

**This page covers growing and organizing your GiftSpark organization — inviting members, building teams and subgroups, and using the directory.** A well-organized membership makes recognition, leaderboards, and insights meaningful at the team level, not just company-wide.

## Inviting members

Admins invite teammates to the organization. New members [sign in with Google](../getting-started/account-setup.md), accept the invite, and land in the organization with the role you assigned. Invites can be shared as links or codes.

## Teams and subgroups

Members are organized into **teams**, and teams can have **subgroups** for finer structure (for example, a department split into squads). This structure powers:

- **Team leaderboards** — friendly competition scoped to a team.
- **Manager insights** — a manager sees their team's engagement, not the whole company's.
- **Targeted recognition and gifting** — activities that make sense at the team level.

## The directory

The directory is a searchable list of everyone in the organization. It's how people find a colleague, see their team, and connect — especially useful for new hires and distributed teams who can't rely on running into each other.

## Use cases

- **Onboarding a new hire** — invite them, place them on the right team, and they immediately appear in the directory.
- **Reorganizing** — move members between teams and subgroups as the org structure changes.
- **Finding the right person** — a teammate uses the directory to find who owns an area.
- **Scoped competition** — team leaderboards keep engagement lively without one giant company-wide ranking.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do people join?**
Through an admin's invite link or code, then Google sign-in.

**Can a member be on more than one team?**
Team and subgroup structure is flexible to match how your organization actually works.

**Is the directory visible to everyone?**
The directory helps members find each other within the organization; profile visibility follows each member's privacy settings.
