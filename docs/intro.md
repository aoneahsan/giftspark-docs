# Introduction

**GiftSpark is an employee engagement platform that combines peer recognition, a redeemable rewards catalog, pulse surveys, gamification, and team gifting into one product that runs on the web, Android, and iOS.** It gives an organization a single place where teammates thank each other publicly, earn points and badges for it, redeem those points for rewards, and stay connected through shared events and gifting.

GiftSpark is built by [Ahsan Mahmood](https://aoneahsan.com). The app is free to use and runs on Firebase. You can open it at [giftspark.aoneahsan.com](https://giftspark.aoneahsan.com) or install the Android app from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aoneahsan.giftspark).

## What GiftSpark does

| Area | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| **Recognition feed** | A shared, searchable feed where teammates post kudos and thank-yous, tag values, and react. |
| **Gamification** | Points, badges, streaks, leaderboards, challenges, and goals that make recognition a habit. |
| **Rewards (Regalos)** | A catalog where points are redeemed for items, with categories, approvals, codes, cart, and orders. |
| **Surveys & insights** | Pulse surveys, sentiment tracking, a manager dashboard, a team health score, and a digest. |
| **Team gifting** | Group gifts, gift circles, registries, wish lists, Secret Santa, and wrapping/DIY ideas. |
| **Events** | Birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and work milestones surfaced on a team calendar. |

Every area shares one account, one organization model, one role/permission system, and one set of analytics — so the recognition you give connects directly to the rewards you can redeem and the insights a manager sees.

## Who it is for

- **People and culture teams** who want recognition to happen daily instead of once a year, with data to show for it.
- **Managers** who want a live read on team morale through surveys, sentiment, and a health score.
- **Every employee** who wants a simple way to say thank you, track teammates' birthdays and milestones, and organize group gifts.
- **Organizations of any size** — GiftSpark supports multiple organizations with role-based access, so a company, a department, or a small team can each run their own space.

## What it is not

Honesty matters more than marketing. A few explicit non-claims:

- **Not a payroll or HRIS system.** GiftSpark complements your HR tools; it does not replace them or store payroll data.
- **Not a paid-only product.** The core platform is free and works without any paid integration. Optional card payments use Stripe when configured, and fall back to an admin-managed flow when not.
- **Not a closed silo.** Recognition can flow to Slack and Microsoft Teams, and the companion browser extension lets people save gift ideas outside the app.
