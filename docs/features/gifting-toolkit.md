# Gifting Toolkit

**GiftSpark's gifting toolkit is a set of tools for organizing real gifts among teammates — group gifts, gift circles, registries, wish lists, Secret Santa draws, and wrapping and DIY inspiration.** It handles the coordination that usually happens in scattered chat threads and spreadsheets.

## Group gifts

Group gifts let several people **chip in together** on a single present — for a birthday, a farewell, or a milestone. The toolkit tracks who is contributing so the organizer isn't chasing people manually.

## Gift circles

A gift circle is a recurring group of people who gift to each other on a schedule or for ongoing occasions. It's a lightweight way to keep a team's gifting organized over time rather than re-forming the group every event.

## Registries and wish lists

- **Gift registry** — a curated list someone publishes for an occasion, so gifts land on target.
- **Wish lists** — ongoing lists of things a person would like, which makes choosing a gift for them easy and reduces duplicate or unwanted gifts.

## Secret Santa

The Secret Santa tool runs the classic draw: participants join, the tool assigns each person a recipient privately, and everyone knows who they're gifting without spoiling the surprise. It removes the manual drawing of names and the awkwardness of someone seeing their own assignment.

## Wrapping and DIY ideas

For the last mile, the toolkit offers **wrapping ideas** and **DIY project** inspiration — practical touches that make a gift feel considered without needing a separate search.

## Use cases

- **A team farewell** — the organizer starts a group gift and teammates contribute toward one meaningful present.
- **Holiday season** — a department runs a Secret Santa draw with a set budget.
- **Hard-to-shop-for teammate** — their wish list removes the guesswork.
- **Recurring gifting** — a gift circle keeps a close-knit team's exchanges organized year-round.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Secret Santa reveal assignments to the organizer?**
Assignments are made privately to keep the surprise; participants see only their own recipient.

**Can multiple people contribute to one gift?**
Yes — that's exactly what group gifts are for, with contribution tracking built in.

**Are wish lists visible to my whole organization?**
Wish lists are meant to help teammates choose gifts for you; visibility follows your profile and sharing settings.
