# Recognition & Feed

**The recognition feed is the heart of GiftSpark — a shared, public stream where teammates post kudos and thank-yous, tag company values, and react to each other's wins.** It turns appreciation from a private message into a visible, searchable record that the whole organization can see and build on.

## Kudos and thank-yous

A recognition is a short, specific message from one person to one or more teammates. When you give recognition you can:

- Recognize **one teammate or several** at once.
- Tag a **company value** (for example, "Ownership" or "Customer first") so recognition reinforces culture.
- Attach **points** that count toward the recipient's rewards balance.
- Add detail about exactly what they did, so the praise is concrete.

## The social feed

Every recognition lands in the organization feed, where teammates can **react** and **comment**. This visibility is the point: seeing peers recognized models the behavior and makes appreciation contagious. The feed is browsable and searchable, so you can revisit who was recognized for what.

## Values-based recognition

Tagging recognition with a value links day-to-day appreciation to the behaviors your organization wants more of. Over time this produces a record of which values show up most — useful context for the [surveys and insights](surveys-and-insights.md) area.

## Related recognition tools

GiftSpark groups several lightweight appreciation surfaces alongside the main feed:

- **Kudos** — quick, low-friction shout-outs.
- **Thank you** — a focused way to express gratitude for a specific act.
- **Wall of Fame** — a curated highlight of standout recognition.
- **Spotlight** — features a person or team for a period.

## Use cases

- **Daily appreciation** — a teammate unblocks your work, so you post a thank-you tagged with "Teamwork" and a few points.
- **Onboarding a culture** — a new hire reads the feed and immediately sees which behaviors get celebrated.
- **Manager visibility** — a manager scans the feed to spot quietly excellent contributors who rarely self-promote.
- **Closing a project** — the team posts a round of recognition naming exactly who carried which part.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is recognition public?**
Yes — recognition posts to the organization feed by design, because visibility is what makes peer recognition work. Profile fields like your birthday remain private unless you choose to share them.

**Do I need points to recognize someone?**
No. You can post recognition without attaching points; points are an optional reinforcement that ties into [rewards](regalos-and-rewards.md).

**Can I recognize more than one person at once?**
Yes — recognition supports multiple recipients in a single post.

**Where do the points go?**
Attached points count toward the recipient's balance, which they redeem in the [Regalos rewards catalog](regalos-and-rewards.md).
