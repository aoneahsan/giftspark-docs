# Shopping & Discovery

**GiftSpark's shopping and discovery tools help you choose, find, and track the right gift — with product comparison, a store locator, shopping lists, size and style guides, compatibility checks, and recommendations.** They take the guesswork out of "what should I get them, and where?"

## Recommendations

The recommendations engine suggests gifts based on the signals GiftSpark already has — interests, style, and occasion. It's a starting point that turns a blank page into a shortlist. For a more interactive experience, the [AI features](ai-features.md) build on the same idea.

## Product comparison

When you're torn between options, product comparison lays choices side by side so the trade-offs are clear instead of held in your head across browser tabs.

## Store locator

The store locator helps you find where to buy something nearby, using location features so a digital shortlist turns into a real purchase.

## Lists and guides

- **Shopping lists** — keep what you intend to buy organized in one place.
- **Size guide** — avoid the most common gift mistake by getting sizing right.
- **Style profile** — capture someone's taste so suggestions land closer to the mark.
- **Compatibility** — check that a gift fits the recipient before you commit.

## Delivery tracker

Once a gift is on its way, the delivery tracker helps you keep an eye on it, so you know whether it'll arrive in time for the occasion.

## Use cases

- **Decision paralysis** — product comparison narrows three options to one.
- **Last-minute, in-person** — the store locator finds a nearby shop that has the item today.
- **Getting size right** — the size guide prevents the classic wrong-size gift.
- **Matching taste** — a style profile steers suggestions toward what the recipient actually likes.

## Honest framing

These tools help you decide and find; GiftSpark is not itself a store and does not sell or ship the products. Recommendations are suggestions based on available signals, not guarantees that a gift will be loved.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does GiftSpark sell the products it recommends?**
No. The shopping tools help you choose and locate items; the purchase happens with the retailer.

**How are recommendations generated?**
From the interests, style, and occasion signals you provide. The more complete a profile, the better the suggestions.
