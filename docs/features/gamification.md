# Gamification

**GiftSpark's gamification layer makes recognition habitual by rewarding participation with points, badges, streaks, leaderboards, challenges, and goals.** Instead of relying on willpower, it gives people small, visible reasons to keep recognizing teammates and engaging with the platform.

## Points

Points are the shared currency of GiftSpark. Members earn them by giving and receiving recognition and by participating in activities, and they spend them in the [rewards catalog](regalos-and-rewards.md). Your current balance is always visible on your profile.

## Badges

Badges are awarded for milestones and behaviors — for example, sustained recognition, hitting a streak, or completing a challenge. They give people a sense of progress beyond a raw point total and a way to show what they've contributed.

## Streaks

A streak tracks consecutive periods of participation. Keeping a streak alive is a gentle nudge to come back and recognize someone today rather than letting weeks pass between thank-yous.

## Leaderboards

Leaderboards rank members and teams by points and activity over a period. They create friendly competition and surface the people keeping recognition alive. Leaderboards can be viewed across the organization or scoped to a team.

## Challenges and goals

- **Challenges** are time-boxed activities that invite people to participate toward a shared target — a good way to kick-start engagement.
- **Goals** let individuals or teams set a target and track progress toward it.

## Use cases

- **Reviving a quiet feed** — launch a one-week recognition challenge to get everyone posting again.
- **Recognizing consistency** — a streak badge rewards the person who shows up to appreciate teammates every week.
- **Team rivalry** — two departments race on the leaderboard during a busy quarter.
- **Personal targets** — a team lead sets a goal to recognize every team member at least once a month.

## How gamification connects to rewards

Gamification is not the end in itself — it feeds the loop. Points earned through these mechanics are redeemed in [Regalos](regalos-and-rewards.md), and participation patterns become signals in the [surveys and insights](surveys-and-insights.md) area. The aim is durable engagement, not vanity metrics.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can leaderboards be turned off or scoped?**
Leaderboards can be viewed organization-wide or per team, so competition stays at a level that fits your culture.

**Do points expire?**
Point handling follows your organization's configuration; admins manage the rewards economy from the admin area.

**Are badges customizable?**
Badges recognize built-in milestones and behaviors today; the set grows as the product evolves.
