# AI Features

**GiftSpark's AI features help you find the right gift faster — an AI gift finder, a chat assistant, an interest graph, and recommendations that turn what you know about a person into concrete suggestions.** They sit on top of the [shopping and discovery](shopping-and-discovery.md) tools and make the choosing step conversational.

## AI gift finder

The AI gift finder takes the details you have — the recipient, the occasion, a budget, and any interests — and proposes gift ideas. Instead of browsing endlessly, you describe the situation and get a shortlist to react to.

## Chat assistant

The AI chat assistant lets you ask for gift help in plain language. It's useful when you don't know where to start: describe the person and the moment, and refine the suggestions through conversation.

## Interest graph

The interest graph maps what a person is into — connecting interests so suggestions can branch from "likes coffee" to adjacent ideas a flat list would miss. It's the structure that makes recommendations feel considered rather than random.

## Recommendations

The recommendations surface in the AI area shares its foundation with the broader [recommendations](shopping-and-discovery.md) engine, applying the interest graph and profile signals to suggest gifts tuned to the recipient and occasion.

## Use cases

- **Blank-page problem** — you have no idea what to get, so you describe the person to the gift finder and start from its shortlist.
- **Refining a vague idea** — you chat with the assistant to narrow "something for a coworker who likes plants" into specific options.
- **Going beyond the obvious** — the interest graph suggests an adjacent idea you wouldn't have thought of.
- **Occasion-aware picks** — recommendations factor in the occasion, not just the person.

## Honest framing

GiftSpark's AI features are assistants, not oracles. They generate suggestions from the information you give them; the quality of the ideas depends on the quality of the details and your own judgment about the recipient. Treat the output as a helpful starting point you curate, not a final answer.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do AI features cost extra?**
The AI features are part of the GiftSpark experience; you don't pay separately to use them.

**How good are the suggestions?**
They're as good as the inputs. A recipient with a filled-in profile and clear occasion yields sharper ideas than a one-line prompt.

**Do they replace the shopping tools?**
No — they complement them. Use the AI finder to decide, then the [store locator and comparison tools](shopping-and-discovery.md) to act.
