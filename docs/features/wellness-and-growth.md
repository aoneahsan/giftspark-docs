# Wellness & Growth

**GiftSpark's wellness and growth area supports the whole person — wellness and mental-health resources, mentorship, learning, skills, and casual connection tools like coffee roulette.** Engagement isn't only recognition; it's also feeling supported and able to grow.

## Wellness and mental health

The **wellness** and **mental health** sections collect resources and tools that help people look after themselves. They're a place to surface support rather than leave it buried in a wiki nobody reads.

## Mentorship

The mentorship tool helps pair people for guidance and growth, connecting those who want to learn with those who can share experience. Structured mentorship is one of the highest-leverage growth tools a team has, and GiftSpark gives it a home.

## Learning and lunch-and-learns

- **Learning** — a space for development resources and growth content.
- **Lunch & learn** — organize informal sessions where teammates teach each other, keeping knowledge-sharing lightweight and social.

## Skills

The skills area helps capture and surface what people can do, which makes it easier to find the right person for a task, build mentor pairings, and spot growth opportunities.

## Coffee roulette

Coffee roulette randomly pairs teammates for an informal chat. It's a simple, proven way to build cross-team relationships — especially valuable for distributed teams where people rarely bump into each other.

## Use cases

- **Onboarding connections** — coffee roulette introduces a new hire to people outside their immediate team.
- **Growing a junior teammate** — mentorship pairs them with an experienced colleague.
- **Spreading knowledge** — a lunch-and-learn turns one person's expertise into the whole team's.
- **Supporting wellbeing** — the wellness section puts mental-health resources one tap away.

## Honest framing

GiftSpark's wellness and mental-health features point people toward resources and connection; they are not a clinical or medical service. For health concerns, encourage people to reach out to a qualified professional or your organization's support channels.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is coffee roulette mandatory?**
No — it's an opt-in way to meet teammates, not a requirement.

**Do the wellness resources replace an EAP or professional support?**
No. They complement, not replace, professional support and any program your organization offers.
