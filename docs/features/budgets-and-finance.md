# Budgets & Finance

**GiftSpark's finance area has two halves: organizational budgets that keep the rewards economy under control, and personal money helpers that make gifting and spending smarter.** Together they answer "what can we afford?" for admins and "how do I spend wisely?" for members.

## Recognition budgets

Admins set and track budgets for recognition and rewards so the points economy stays sustainable. The **budget planner** helps map spending across a period, and budget views show how much has been allocated and used. This keeps the [rewards catalog](regalos-and-rewards.md) from drifting out of control.

## Personal money helpers

Around the organizational budget, GiftSpark bundles a set of optional, everyday money tools that are useful when buying gifts or managing spending:

- **Cashback** — track cashback opportunities.
- **Coupons and discounts** — keep useful savings in one place.
- **Price alerts** — get notified when something you're watching changes price.
- **Receipt organizer** — keep receipts tidy and findable.
- **Tax calculator** — a quick helper for tax math.
- **FSA guide** — guidance on flexible spending accounts.

These tools are conveniences layered on top of the core recognition product; you can use as many or as few as you like.

## Use cases

- **Staying on budget** — an admin uses the budget planner to allocate recognition spend across quarters and watches usage against it.
- **Smarter gift buying** — a member checks price alerts and coupons before purchasing a teammate's gift.
- **Tidy expenses** — the receipt organizer keeps gift receipts in one place for later reference.
- **Quick math** — the tax calculator answers a one-off question without leaving the app.

## Honest framing

The personal finance helpers are convenience tools, not professional financial advice. The tax calculator and FSA guide are informational; for real financial or tax decisions, consult a qualified professional.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are the finance tools required?**
No. Recognition budgets matter to admins, but the personal money helpers are optional extras you can ignore.

**Does GiftSpark handle real payments here?**
The finance area is about planning and convenience. Actual card payments, when enabled, run through Stripe in the [rewards](regalos-and-rewards.md) flow.
