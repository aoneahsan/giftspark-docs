# Events & Calendar

**GiftSpark's events area keeps your team's important dates — birthdays, work anniversaries, holidays, life events, and milestones — on one shared calendar with reminders, so celebrations never slip by unnoticed.** Remembering the right moment is half of recognition, and this is the tooling that makes it automatic.

## Birthdays and anniversaries

Members can add their **birthday** and **work anniversary** (with control over visibility). GiftSpark surfaces upcoming dates so teammates can recognize, gift, or simply say happy birthday at the right time instead of finding out a week late.

## Holidays

The holidays view keeps shared and organizational holidays in front of the team, which helps with planning and gives a natural cadence for seasonal recognition and gifting.

## Life events and milestones

Beyond the regular calendar, GiftSpark tracks **life events** (the meaningful personal moments people choose to share) and work **milestones**. A **memory timeline** gives a longer view of a person's or team's notable moments over time.

## Family calendar

A **family calendar** view helps people keep personal occasions organized alongside work events, so a single place holds the dates that matter.

## How events connect to the rest of GiftSpark

Events are a trigger for the rest of the platform: an upcoming birthday is a cue to post [recognition](recognition-and-feed.md) or organize a [group gift](gifting-toolkit.md), and milestones are natural moments for a [spotlight](recognition-and-feed.md). The calendar doesn't sit in isolation — it prompts action.

## Use cases

- **No more missed birthdays** — the team sees upcoming birthdays and organizes a card or group gift in advance.
- **Celebrating tenure** — work-anniversary reminders prompt recognition for long-serving teammates.
- **Seasonal planning** — the holidays view helps a manager plan around time off and seasonal activities.
- **Honoring milestones** — a life event a teammate shares becomes a moment of support.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are my personal dates public?**
You control the visibility of dates like your birthday; events are designed to help teammates celebrate you, not to expose private details.

**Does GiftSpark remind me about upcoming dates?**
Upcoming events are surfaced in the app, and push notifications on mobile can keep them top of mind.
