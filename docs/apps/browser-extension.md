# Browser Extension

**The GiftSpark browser extension is a companion that lets you save gift ideas from any website with one click, auto-detect product details, and track events and budgets — syncing to your account through Google sign-in.** It captures gift inspiration where you find it, outside the main app.

## What it does

- **Save gift ideas** — one click saves an item you're viewing on any site.
- **Smart product detection** — automatically extracts the product name, price, and image so you don't retype.
- **Right-click to save** — a context-menu action saves from anywhere on a page.
- **Event reminders** — keep birthdays and anniversaries in view so a saved idea turns into a timely gift.
- **Budget tracking** — monitor gift spending by recipient.
- **Search and organize** — filter saved ideas and tag them by recipient.
- **Sync** — sign in with Google to keep saved ideas with your account.

## Supported browsers

The extension is built with the WXT framework and React, targeting **Chrome, Firefox, and Edge** (with a conversion path for Safari). It uses the same Google account as the rest of GiftSpark.

## How it fits with the app

The extension is the capture surface; the [app](mobile-app.md) is where gifting comes together. Save an idea while browsing, then organize it into a [wish list, registry, or group gift](../features/gifting-toolkit.md) inside GiftSpark. The [events calendar](../features/events-and-calendar.md) reminds you when it's time to act on what you saved.

## Use cases

- **Inspiration capture** — you spot a perfect gift while browsing and save it before you forget.
- **Shopping across sites** — collect candidates from several stores, then compare them later.
- **Budget discipline** — track what you're spending per recipient as you save ideas.
- **Occasion-ready** — saved ideas plus event reminders mean you're never scrambling last minute.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which browsers are supported?**
Chrome, Firefox, and Edge, with a Safari conversion path.

**Do I need a GiftSpark account to use it?**
You can save ideas, and signing in with Google syncs them to your account across devices.

**Does it work on any website?**
Yes — the one-click and right-click save work on arbitrary pages, with automatic detection of product details where available.
